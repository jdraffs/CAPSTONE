About Me

Sheryl ‘Sharima’ Renomeron-Morales is currently an Associate Professor and faculty researcher at the Research Management Office of the Polytechnic University of the Philippines. Her research primarily focuses on curriculum and instruction, higher education peace and security studies, and inclusive education. She also serves as an adviser to the National Youth Congress of the Philippines 2013, a youth organization dedicated to the promotion of youth, peace, and nationalism.
